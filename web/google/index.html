Open Google Calendar
  1. In Google Calendar, click Add.
  2. On your Android phone, open the Google Calendar app › ☰ › Settings. Under your account (tap Show more if needed), tap Hawks Lunch (it may be called Hellgate Lunch or hellgatelunch.github.io) and turn on Sync.
  3. Back in ☰, make sure that calendar is ticked, then tap Refresh.

Each school day then shows "Lunch: …" in your calendar, with breakfast and the middle school break in the details. It updates on its own.
