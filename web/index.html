    Get it in your calendar

    Shows each day's lunch in your phone's calendar and its widgets. Updates on its own.

    iPhone

    Google only lets you add a calendar from a computer. It's a one-time setup:

    1. On a computer signed in to Google, go to hellgatelunch.github.io/google and click Add.
    2. On your Android phone, open the Google Calendar app › ☰ › Settings. Under your account (tap Show more if needed), tap Hawks Lunch (it may be called Hellgate Lunch or hellgatelunch.github.io) and turn on Sync.
    3. Back in ☰, make sure that calendar is ticked, then tap Refresh. Events can take a few minutes to appear.
